- **[low / confidence: medium]** `skills/init-project/SKILL.md:62`, `:87` and `:106` (Steps 4, 5 and 6): other writes in this skill follow a committed symlink. They are pre-existing, not introduced by the fixes, but they are the same mechanism the fixes close.
