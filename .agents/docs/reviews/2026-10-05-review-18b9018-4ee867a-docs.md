## Findings

- **[medium / confidence: high]** `README.md:153` — "It tells stale from fresh, never genuine from forged — it is printed by the very binary in question; authenticity is the signature and checksum on the file, before the move." There is no signature any more (ADR-053); the same file says at README.md:97 that the checksums are not signed and prove only that the download is whole. Fix: "…never genuine from forged; the checksum on the file, before the move, shows the download is whole, and who made it rests on your GitHub credential and HTTPS."
- **[medium / confidence: high]** `.agents/docs/deploy/remote.md:183` → `.agents/docs/deploy/remote.md:156` — the "After the upload" paragraph says `gh release view` "lists the six assets"; step 4 (line 156) says five, six only for v2.4.0.
