4. **[low / confidence: high]** `.agents/docs/testing/kit-preamble.sh:45-46` (header `:14-15`, comment `:43-44`). The same claim is restated at `.agents/docs/testing/guide.md:178` and `.agents/docs/bugs/BUG-157-kit-preamble-commit-assert-141.md:29`.

   - **Probed** with the shipped lines 39-47:
