## Context

The operator asked whether the harness wastes turns and tokens. A headless A/B build of a CRM booking service, with the same prompt and model, measured three arms:

| Arm | Cost | Wall time | Acceptance test |
|---|---|---|---|
| No harness | $0.54 | 2.4 min | 25/25 |
| Harness loaded | $1.11 | 5.0 min | 25/25 |
| Harness, full process | $8.46 | 30.8 min | 25/25 |

A blind judge ranked full-process first on robustness, harness-loaded first as the fit for a simple system, and no-harness last. The operator approved recommendations 1–6 and 8, and struck 7.
